# Bitcoin-Only & Bitcoin-First Exchanges for Indonesian Citizens

*Research compiled: 2026-02-16*

---

## Summary

There are very few true Bitcoin-only exchanges that explicitly support Indonesian citizens. Most Bitcoin-only platforms are restricted to the US, Canada, Europe, or Australia. Indonesian users seeking Bitcoin-only options are largely limited to: (1) decentralized P2P platforms like Bisq, Hodl Hodl, and RoboSats that operate globally without geographic restrictions, (2) Luno, which supports Indonesia and has a limited crypto selection heavily weighted toward Bitcoin, or (3) general Indonesian exchanges like Indodax, Pintu, Tokocrypto, Reku, and Upbit Indonesia that support Bitcoin but also list hundreds of altcoins. Strike may support Indonesia as part of its 95+ country expansion but this is unconfirmed.

Among **Indonesian local exchanges**, the landscape as of December 2025 includes 25 fully OJK-licensed platforms (PAKD) and 4 prospective platforms (CPAKD). The largest by market share is **Upbit Indonesia** (35% market share, South Korean-backed). **Reku** (2M+ users, founded 2017, main sponsor of Indonesia Bitcoin Conference 2023, turned profitable in 2024) and **Pintu** (9M+ downloads, $150M+ funding from Pantera/Coinbase Ventures/Lightspeed) are major locally-founded platforms with strong app ratings. **Indodax** remains the oldest (founded 2014) with 9.6M+ users. All support BTC/IDR trading.

**Binance**, the world's largest crypto exchange (300M+ users), is accessible from Indonesia and launched BTC/IDR direct trading in November 2025. It operates locally through its subsidiary Tokocrypto (BAPPEBTI-licensed). Among **Singapore/regional exchanges**, Coinhako supports Indonesia with IDR trading (since 2018) but has very poor user reviews. Independent Reserve (Singapore/Australia) does not support IDR. Tokenize Xchange lost its Singapore license in 2025 and lacks Indonesian regulatory approval. Zipmex has ceased all operations and is defunct.

## Section 2: Decentralized/P2P Bitcoin-Only Exchanges (Globally Accessible)

These platforms operate without geographic restrictions and are the most accessible Bitcoin-only options for Indonesian citizens.

### 11. Bisq

- **URL:** https://bisq.network
- **Type:** Bitcoin-only (decentralized P2P)
- **Founded:** 2014 by Manfred Karrer (originally called "Bitsquare")
- **Headquarters:** Decentralized (no company, no servers)
- **Indonesia Support:** YES (technically). Bisq is a decentralized desktop application; anyone worldwide can use it. Supports 40+ fiat currencies. IDR (Indonesian Rupiah) support depends on finding counterparties willing to trade in IDR.
- **Relative Size:** Small-medium. Low liquidity compared to centralized exchanges. Trade limits are initially low for new users.
- **User Reviews:**
  - Trustpilot: ~4.3/5 (few reviews)
- **Notable Features:** Fully decentralized (no central server), no KYC, Tor integration by default, open source, multisig escrow, desktop software only (no mobile app)
- **Restrictions:** Requires desktop computer (Windows/Mac/Linux). Low liquidity, especially for IDR pairs. Initial trade limits are very low. Slower than centralized exchanges. Requires BTC deposit for security.

## Key Takeaways for Indonesian Bitcoin Buyers

1. **No major Bitcoin-only exchange explicitly confirms Indonesia support.** The Bitcoin-only exchange ecosystem is overwhelmingly focused on the US, Canada, Europe, and Australia.

2. **Best Bitcoin-only options for Indonesia (if they work):**
   - **Strike** -- Most likely to work in Indonesia given its 95+ country reach, but unconfirmed. Check the app store.
   - **CoinCorner** -- 45+ countries, possibly including Indonesia. Check their website.
   - **Amber** -- Wide international reach, but Indonesia not listed among Asian countries.

3. **Best Bitcoin-only P2P options (definitely accessible from Indonesia):**
   - **Bisq** -- Desktop software, fully decentralized, no KYC. Low IDR liquidity.
   - **Hodl Hodl** -- Web-based P2P, no KYC, multisig escrow. Low IDR liquidity.
   - **RoboSats** -- Lightning-native, Tor-only, maximum privacy. Very low IDR liquidity.

4. **Best Indonesian exchanges for buying Bitcoin (not Bitcoin-only):**
   - **Upbit Indonesia** -- Largest market share in Indonesia (35%). Excellent app store ratings (iOS 4.7, Play 4.6). Spot-only trading makes it simple for Bitcoin buy-and-hold. 98% cold storage. Backed by South Korean tech giant Dunamu/Kakao. Downside: higher fees (0.23-0.51%) compared to competitors.
   - **Reku** -- Founded 2017, rebranded 2022. 2M+ users. Main sponsor of Indonesia Bitcoin Conference 2023 (including a Lightning Network hackathon). Turned profitable in 2024 with 50%+ margins. Zero-fee Lightning Mode for instant Bitcoin purchases. Backed by AC Ventures, Coinbase Ventures, Skystar Capital ($11M Series A). Google Play 4.5/5 with 41,900+ reviews. Very low fees (0% Lightning Mode, 0.1% Pro Mode). Also offers US stock/ETF trading. Downside: 150+ altcoins, no Lightning Network withdrawals despite sponsoring a Lightning hackathon.
   - **Pintu** -- Founded 2020. 9M+ downloads. Best-funded Indonesian crypto startup ($150M+ total funding from Pantera Capital, Coinbase Ventures, Lightspeed, Northstar). 320+ assets. iOS 4.7/5 with 50K+ reviews. DCA features with simulations. Competitive fees (0.12% maker / 0.17% taker). Had representatives at Indonesia Bitcoin Conference 2025. Downside: 320+ altcoins, Web3 wallet discontinued.
   - **Luno** -- Closest to "Bitcoin-first" among exchanges available in Indonesia. Only 27 assets, IDR support, good Trustpilot reviews (4.0/5). International platform (London HQ, 40+ countries).
   - **Indodax** -- Oldest Indonesian exchange (founded 2014 as Bitcoin.co.id). 9.6M users. IDR pairs. But 450+ altcoins and mixed Trustpilot reviews.

5. **Binance as a major option (not Bitcoin-only):**
   - **Binance** is the world's largest crypto exchange (300M+ users, $34T annual volume) and is accessible from Indonesia. BTC/IDR direct trading launched November 29, 2025. However, Binance itself is not BAPPEBTI-registered -- it operates in Indonesia through its subsidiary Tokocrypto. The global Binance app works in Indonesia but exists in a regulatory gray area. Trustpilot reviews are very poor (1.6/5) despite strong third-party review scores. Best for experienced traders who want maximum liquidity and the lowest fees.

6. **Singapore/Regional exchanges for Indonesia:**
   - **Coinhako** (Singapore) -- Officially supports Indonesia with IDR trading since 2018, MAS-licensed in Singapore, but has extremely poor user reviews (Trustpilot 1.2/5) and limited crypto selection.
   - **Independent Reserve** (Singapore/Australia) -- Strong exchange recently acquired by IG Group, but does NOT support IDR and Indonesia is not confirmed as a supported country.
   - **Tokenize Xchange** -- Lost its Singapore license in 2025 and relocated to Malaysia. Offers IDR payments via third-party gateways but lacks Indonesian regulatory approval. Not recommended.
   - **Zipmex** -- DEFUNCT. Ceased all Indonesia operations. Do not use.
   - Overall, the Singapore exchange ecosystem offers limited value for Indonesian Bitcoin buyers compared to local Indonesian exchanges or the global Binance platform.

7. **Indonesian regulatory environment:** Since January 10, 2025, crypto is regulated as "digital financial assets" under OJK (Financial Services Authority), which took over from BAPPEBTI per Government Regulation Number 49 Year 2024. As of December 2025, OJK published a whitelist of 29 licensed crypto platforms (25 fully licensed PAKD + 4 prospective CPAKD). All existing BAPPEBTI licenses remain valid. Key licensed platforms include Indodax, Pintu, Reku, Upbit Indonesia, Tokocrypto, Pluang, Nanovest, Stockbit Crypto, Ajaib, Triv, Bittime, and others. Luno Indonesia is among 4 platforms still in the prospective licensing (CPAKD) stage. Bitcoin can be traded but not used as a payment instrument. All trading providers must fully comply with OJK Reg. 27/2024 by July 2025.

8. **OJK-Licensed Indonesian Exchanges (December 2025 Whitelist):** The full list of 25 fully licensed PAKD platforms includes: Ajaib, ASTAL, Bittime, Bitwewe, Bitwyre, BTSE Indonesia, Coinvest, CoinX, CYRA, Floq, Indodax, Koinsayang, MAKS, Mobee, Naga Exchange, Nanovest, Nobi, Pintu, Pluang, Reku, Samuel Kripto, Stockbit Crypto, Tokocrypto, Triv, and Upbit Indonesia. Four additional platforms are in the prospective stage (CPAKD): digitalexchange.id, Fasset, GudangKripto, and Luno.

9. **Strategy for a Bitcoin-only approach in Indonesia:** Use an OJK/BAPPEBTI-regulated Indonesian exchange to convert IDR to Bitcoin, then immediately withdraw to self-custody (hardware wallet). This gives you the regulatory compliance of a local exchange with the self-sovereignty of holding your own keys. Recommended exchanges for this strategy:
   - **For lowest fees:** Reku Lightning Mode (0% fee) or Reku/Pintu Pro Mode (0.1-0.12%)
   - **For maximum liquidity:** Binance/Tokocrypto (largest global exchange, BTC/IDR since Nov 2025)
   - **For simplicity and security:** Upbit Indonesia (largest Indonesian market share, spot-only, 98% cold storage)
   - **For Bitcoin-first ethos:** Luno (only 27 assets, less altcoin temptation) or Reku (sponsored Indonesia Bitcoin Conference, Lightning Network hackathon)
   - **For regulatory clarity:** Any of the 25 fully licensed PAKD platforms on the OJK whitelist
