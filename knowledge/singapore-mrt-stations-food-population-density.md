# Top 10 Singapore MRT Stations by Restaurants & Population Density (Walking Distance)

*Research compiled: 2026-02-17*

## Summary

This ranking combines **population density** (residents per km² within the station's planning area) with **restaurant/food outlet concentration** (hawker centres, food courts, coffeeshops, restaurants) within walking distance (~500m) of each MRT station. Stations are ordered from most to least favorable on the combined metric.

## Methodology & Caveats

- **Population density** was calculated from 2025 estimates (citypopulation.de) — population / planning area in km².
- **Restaurant density** is qualitative, based on known hawker centres (stall counts where available), mall food courts, and restaurant corridor presence from multiple Singapore food guides.
- No single authoritative dataset ranks all 100+ MRT stations by restaurant count within a radius. This ranking synthesizes the best available data.
- Stations like **Dhoby Ghaut**, **City Hall**, **Raffles Place**, and **Clarke Quay** have high restaurant density but sit in commercial planning areas (Downtown Core, Museum, etc.) with very low resident populations — so they scored lower on the combined metric.
- Conversely, stations like **Punggol** and **Woodlands** have high population density but fewer dining options per capita than the stations listed above.
